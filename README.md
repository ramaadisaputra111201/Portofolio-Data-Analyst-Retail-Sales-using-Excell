# Portofolio-Data-Analyst-Retail-Sales-using-Excell
In this project, I analyzed retail sales data using Microsoft Excel to identify sales trends, top-performing products, and customer purchasing patterns. The process involved data cleaning, creating pivot tables, using lookup and statistical functions, and developing interactive dashboards with charts and slicers.

The data used consists of 200 retail sales records with 10 variables, namely: Transaction ID, Date, Customer Name, Product, Category, Quantity, Unit Price, Total Price, Payment Method, and Store Location.


Data Cleaning :
1. Calculation error: Total Price for Transaction ID T0042 was incorrect it should be Rp 50.000.000 (corrected).
2. Date format: date field was malformed and has been converted to a proper date type.

Pivot Table, Visualization, and Result :
1. The total store revenue from January to March 2024 was Rp 793,545,000.
2. March recorded the highest revenue, amounting to Rp 286,590,000.
3. The most frequently purchased product was the Desk Chair, with 83 units sold across all store locations.
4. The most commonly used payment method was Cash, with 56 transactions.

Problem Solving & Business Insight :
1. Promote Desk Chair, which contributes a significant portion of total sales. Additionally, promotions such as bundling with Desk Lamp or multi-item discounts are likely to boost sales, as these products show strong demand.
2. There is an upward trend in revenue from January to March 2024, with cash payments still being the dominant method and limited bundling transactions (mostly one product per invoice). Based on these patterns, it is recommended to run end-of-quarter promotions such as discounts or bundling offers, and provide incentives for E-wallet/Card payments to encourage cashless transactions, accelerate reconciliation, reduce cash-handling risks, and facilitate digital transaction analysis.
